GOOD TO KNOW

Let’s make your order easy.

A few details upfront help us quote accurately and deliver your project on time.

Ask a question
How is my final price calculated?

Your final price is calculated based on the selected size, quantity, material, and finishing. You may proceed with your inquiry using these specifications.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept PDF, AI, PSD, PNG, and JPG files. For best results, send print-ready PDFs in CMYK with bleed included.

How long will my order take?

Most orders can be completed within the same day, depending on the submission time, quantity, and finishing requirements. Orders submitted before 3:00 PM may be ready within the day, while orders submitted at 3:00 PM onwards will be available the following day.

Do you offer rush printing?

Yes. Rush options are available for select products. Contact us with your deadline and we’ll confirm what’s possible.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash and GCash payments. If you choose Cash, you must pay 50% of the total order price through GCash on our website as a down payment, while the remaining 50% will be paid in cash at our shop upon collection. If you choose GCash, you must pay the full 100% of the order price through GCash on our website.

Can you help adjust or create my design?

Yes! We can make adjustments to your existing design or create a new one from scratch. Minor adjustments are free of charge, while major adjustments and custom designs from scratch have a fixed design fee of ₱150. Once the design is prepared, we will send a digital proof to your registered email address for review and approval. Production will only begin once you have approved the design.

Do you deliver?

We do not provide direct delivery services. However, you may arrange your own delivery through third-party couriers such as Lalamove, Grab, or other available delivery services. The delivery booking and fees will be shouldered by the customer. If you wish to have your order delivered, please notify us once you have booked a rider so we can coordinate the pickup and handover.

OUR STORY

Built around the belief that
every detail matters.

Established in 2017 in Binangonan, Rizal, QTA Printing Services started as Quality Tarp Advertising, focusing on quality tarpaulin, sticker, and document printing using Roland printing machines. Over the years, the business expanded its services, introducing signage production in 2019 to meet growing customer needs.

Today, QTA Printing Services offers nearly 20 printing and related services for individuals, businesses, and organizations. With years of experience in the printing industry, QTA continues to grow while maintaining its commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Our mission

QTA Printing Services aims to deliver high-quality, affordable, and reliable printing products and services, while building lasting customer relationships through efficient service, continuous improvement, and dedicated workmanship.

02

Our vision

QTA Printing Services aspires to grow into a well-known and trusted name in the printing industry, delivering consistent quality and solutions that adapt to the changing needs of its customers.
