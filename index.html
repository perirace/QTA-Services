File Preparation & Artwork Specs

What file formats do you accept for printing?

We recommend Vector PDF, AI, PSD, TIFF, or high-resolution JPG/PNG files. For optimal sharpness, all bitmap assets should be set to 300 DPI at 100% actual print size with the color mode configured to CMYK.

For billboard banners larger than 10 feet, 100–150 DPI at actual size is fully sufficient and reduces file upload weight significantly.
Do you check file resolution and bleed before printing?

Yes, our dedicated pre-press team conducts complimentary pre-flight checks on every order. We review image resolution, trim lines, font outlines, and CMYK color breakdowns. Before running the job, we supply a digital PDF proof for your final sign-off.

What is bleed, and why is it required?

Bleed refers to extending the background graphics or color past the final cut trim lines by 2mm to 3mm (0.125 inches). This guarantees that when cutting blades shear through sheets or boards, there are no accidental white hairline borders along the edges.

Orders, Pricing & Payment

How do I get an exact price quotation?

You can use our online rate calculator directly on the Pricing page for instant estimates, or send your specific dimensions and quantities via our inquiry form. Custom commercial quotations are returned same-day within 2–4 business hours.

Is there a minimum order requirement?

We have no minimum order quantity (MOQ) for standard roll-to-roll banners, custom single stickers, and prototype boards. However, significant wholesale volume tiers apply to sticker books, marketing collateral, and bulk signage orders.

What payment methods are accepted?

We currently accept cash and GCash as our available payment methods. Customers may choose whichever of these two payment options is more convenient for them when paying for their orders.
